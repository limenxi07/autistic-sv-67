	This project aims to help you apply the knowledge you’ve gained so far and will acquire throughout this course. In this project, we will see how “classical” AI and ML work together in an AI agent. While not required, you are welcome to explore beyond the course content if you’re interested!

We’re releasing the project early so you can work on it gradually. This way, you can think about it while you’re learning throughout the semester and set expectations for what you can achieve by the end.

Your task is to build an AI agent capable of playing Grid Adventure: Variant 1, a fully observable, single‑agent, deterministic, sequential, static, and discrete game. The game features various gameplay mechanics and image observation.

Refer to: https://grid-universe.github.io/grid-adventure-v1/

To make the grand objective manageable, we provide two tasks, each isolates a subset of challenges, and one final task that test the limits of your agent. Each task includes multiple test cases. Each test case contains multiple level instances that may differ in mechanics, size, and complexity.

Submissions: You will have access to 2 different assessments under problem sets.

    Capstone Project: For submissions, graded, use levels and assets that are not shared with you, max 10 attempts.
    Capstone Project (Debugging): For debugging and testing your agent, not graded, use the same levels and assets that are shared with you, unlimited attempts.

Evaluation: Your grade depends on the performance of your agent in graded test cases. We compare the total reward of the solution of your agent to the optimal total reward. 

Due Date: The due date is the end of the semester, Week 13, 13 November 2026 (Fri), 23:59.
